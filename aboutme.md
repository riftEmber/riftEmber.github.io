## Bio
I've lived in Boise, Idaho since I was 11. It's around that time I started my
first programming project (an attempt at a
[Newgrounds](https://www.newgrounds.com/) clone), and I haven't stopped coding
and experimenting since. I currently work on the compiler for the [Chapel
programming language](https://chapel-lang.org/) at HPE.

## Education
I graduated summa cum laude from Boise State University in 2022, with a B.S. in
Computer Science and Mathematics.

Highlights at BSU:
- member of the [ADaPT Lab](https://boisestate-adaptlab.github.io/),
lead by Dr. Catherine Olschanowsky, working on optimizing compiler research
- programming mentor for BSU's
[FIRST Robotics Team #5871, "The Chickadees"](https://www.boisestate.edu/coen/first-robotics-team-5871/).
- co-founder and vice president of the [Queer STEM Club](https://engage.boisestate.edu/organization/qstemclub)
